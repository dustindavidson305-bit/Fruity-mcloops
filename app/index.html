🎧 My Sounds — Custom Sound Slot

Drop your own sound files here
…or tap IMPORT SOUNDS and pick as many files as you like — a whole pack at once is fine.
This slot accepts just about any sound file: MP3, WAV, OGG, Opus, FLAC, M4A/AAC, AIFF, WebM, MP4 audio, 3GP, AMR, CAF, WMA and more — if your device can decode it, it becomes a playable sound you can preview, rename, delete and drop onto any sequencer track, exactly like a built-in. Imported sounds are saved inside the app (IndexedDB) and are still there after you restart it. MIDI files (.mid/.midi) are note files, not audio — they'll be skipped with a note; export them to WAV or MP3 first. Files your device's codec can't decode are reported, never crash the import.
