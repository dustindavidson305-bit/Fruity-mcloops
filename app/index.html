🎧 Decks A–D — DJ Workstation

Every deck plays at once into the master. Load a different song on each deck and blend.
◀ L R ▶
🍹 Mashup trick: put one song on Deck A in Instrumental stem mode and another on Deck B in Vocals Only mode, match the BPMs (TAP + SYNC), and you get the vocals of one song riding the beat of another. Stem modes are the classic karaoke center-channel trick — they work best on stereo mixes with the vocal mixed in the center; they are not AI separation, and mono files can't be split (the deck will tell you). No headphone cue on a phone's single output — hold a deck's ▶ to solo-preview it is not possible either, so blend by ear like the old days.

🎛️ Master FX Rack (all decks + MP3 one-shot)

Gate chops the deck bus in 1/8-note steps at the sequencer BPM; phaser sweeps a classic 4-stage whoosh. Both are new in v2.2 and save with your project.

⚙ DJ Settings

Settings save automatically on this device. There is no key-lock: moving a deck's pitch fader changes its speed AND pitch together, like real vinyl.

🎹 Sound Library

Every built-in sound is synthesized live by the app — no samples, works fully offline. Tap any sound to preview it, or assign it to a sequencer track below.

🎧 My Sounds — Custom Sound Slot

Drop your own sound files here
…or tap IMPORT SOUNDS and pick as many files as you like — a whole pack at once is fine.
URL grab works with direct links to audio files only. YouTube / SoundCloud / Spotify page links are web pages, not audio files, and won't work — no ripping here. If the site blocks cross-site fetches, download the file first and import it instead.
This slot accepts just about any sound file: MP3, WAV, OGG, Opus, FLAC, M4A/AAC, AIFF, WebM, MP4 audio, 3GP, AMR, CAF, WMA and more — if your device can decode it, it becomes a playable sound you can preview, rename, delete and drop onto any sequencer track, exactly like a built-in. Imported sounds are saved inside the app (IndexedDB) and are still there after you restart it. MIDI files (.mid/.midi) are note files, not audio — they'll be skipped with a note; export them to WAV or MP3 first. Files your device's codec can't decode are reported, never crash the import.

🎹 Pitch & Scale

Root note: C3
Highlighted keys are in the scale. Tap a piano key to hear this track's sound at that note — it also becomes the track's root note. Pitched sounds (bass, synths, keys, pads) play at the root; samples and drums shift pitch in proportion.

🎛️ Mixer — Channel Strips (every sound, every effect)

One strip per sequencer track. Open a strip to shape that sound on its own channel: volume, pan, transpose, 3-band EQ, tone filter + drive, attack/decay, morph, and its own delay & reverb sends. Everything is live while the sequencer plays and saves into your project file. The CH button on a track row jumps straight to its strip.

🥁 Step Sequencer — up to 8 bars, unlimited tracks

BAR 1/4
Loop: 4 bars • 64 steps The grid holds 8 bars (128 steps). Steps past the loop end stay saved, just dimmed — stretch the loop any time and they're still there.
🧰 Bar tools — copy, fill & clear bars (all tracks at once)
→
Fastest way to an 8-bar loop: write bar 1, set the loop to 8 bars, hit FILL, then tweak the later bars so it doesn't just repeat.

💾 Save & Export

Project files save your grid, sounds, tempo, swing and FX settings — but not the MP3 file itself (files can't be stored in the project). Reload your MP3 after loading a project if a track uses the MP3 one-shot. Your work also auto-saves in the app as you go.
